## What's new since 0.3.5

**Voice**
- "Hey Arcee" wake word, trained to your voice, with fewer false wakes; speech is
  understood on this computer.
- Spoken answers in eight named voices (Settings → Voice), "read it out loud", and a
  Read aloud button under each reply. Questions can be answered by voice.

**Memory**
- Arcee remembers lasting facts, and summarises each day and week: ask "what did I
  do yesterday?" or "what did I ask last week?".

**About 60 new things it can do**
- Settings: volume, sound output, brightness, dark mode, Night Light, do not
  disturb, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, text size, lock, sleep, restart, default apps,
  updates.
- Windows: side by side, to another screen or workspace; missed notifications.
- Files and documents: read PDFs, Word, PowerPoint and photos of text; make Word
  documents and PDFs; resize and convert photos; print; recent files; USB drives;
  undo what a request did to files.
- Clipboard, screenshots, screen and mic recording.
- Time: timers, reminders, alarms that ring, repeating reminders, routines that run
  on a schedule, time in other cities, a stopwatch.
- Web: weather up to 16 days, currency, share and crypto prices, driving routes,
  news, train timetables in India (hill stations leg by leg), flight searches.
- Google Calendar and Gmail: read, write, attach, unsubscribe, send a file to
  yourself, find someone's address.
- Spotify: "play Arijit Singh" plays it in the Spotify app, or on open.spotify.com.
- WhatsApp: "send Mom 'home by 8' on WhatsApp", through WhatsApp Web; it asks
  before sending.
- Long commands in the background, and Arcee checking back on something by itself
  later (a page, a file, a command).
- Chrome bookmarks; Flathub app installs without a password; a visible terminal.

**Safety and models**
- Arcee always stops before the final Pay / Place order button and never types
  card numbers, CVVs or a UPI PIN.
- When Groq's free daily allowance is used up (or Groq is down), Arcee carries on
  with the local models on this computer.
