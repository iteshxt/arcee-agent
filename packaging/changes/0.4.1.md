## What's new since 0.4.0

- **Voices at a natural pace.** Every voice used to speak slowly and drawn out; each
  now speaks about 215 words a minute (before: 132 to 196).
- **A portrait for each voice** in Settings → Voice, instead of its initial.
- **Permissions** → "Ask before these too" now covers changing settings, playing
  music, Arcee's own timers, background jobs, editing photos and printing.
- **Models:** the Groq bars turn orange when an allowance runs low and red when it is
  spent, and say when the local models are answering instead.
- After playing music, Arcee no longer opens Spotify again (with "Opening apps" set
  to ask, that left a question waiting on the pill).
